Frequently asked
How long does app review take?
Most submissions get a decision within 48 hours. If your app is rejected, the quality-check feedback appears directly on the app's Overview tab.
Can one organisation have more than one dev store?
No — it's one dev store per organisation by design, to keep the model simple. If your team genuinely needs more, tell us why via email support.
How do I get access to a client's live store?
From Stores → Collaborations, request access with the store's URL and the 4-digit code the merchant gives you. The merchant approves, narrows, or denies the scopes you asked for — never the other way around.
Where do I see what Ratio charges me?
Settings → Billing, once you're signed in, has every invoice with its GST breakdown. See Pricing for the plan structure.
