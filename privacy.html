Privacy Policy

Last updated September 1, 2026

1. What this covers

This policy describes how Ratio handles data about you and your organisation when you use the partner portal. It does not cover how your app handles merchant or shopper data — that is between you and the merchants who install your app, governed by your own privacy policy and the scopes they grant you.

2. Information we collect

  • Account details: name, email, and authentication credentials for everyone in your organisation.
  • Organisation details: legal name, country, billing contact, and payout information.
  • Usage data: API call volume, webhook delivery logs, and extension activity, used for rate limiting, billing, and abuse prevention.

3. How we use it

We use this information to operate the partner program: authenticating your team, enforcing scope and rate-limit boundaries, generating invoices, and reviewing apps for listing. We do not sell partner or organisation data.

4. Collaborator and store access data

When a merchant grants your organisation access to their store, Ratio logs the scopes granted, who approved them, and when they were narrowed or revoked, so both sides have an audit trail. This log is visible to the merchant and to Owners/Admins in your organisation.

5. Data retention

We retain organisation and billing records for as long as required for tax and compliance purposes, even after an organisation is closed. API tokens and store access are revoked immediately on termination.

6. Your rights

You can request a copy of, or deletion of, your personal account data by contacting partners@ratio.win. Organisation-level records tied to billing or compliance may be retained as required by law.

7. Contact

Questions about this policy go to partners@ratio.win.
