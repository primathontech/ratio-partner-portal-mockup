Why partners build on Ratio

The same platform for apps, client stores and the people who work across both.

One dashboard for everything
Apps, dev store, client stores and your team live in a single portal — no separate developer dashboard to context-switch into.
Get paid for what you build
Declare priced plans on your app, and Ratio handles merchant billing, invoicing and your payout — no separate billing integration to build.
Scoped access, not shared passwords
Request time-boxed, scoped access to a client's store with a code the merchant controls. Every action is attributed to the person who did it.
Ship with confidence
Every submission goes through review, with feedback in the portal — not by email. Roll back to any prior version at any time.

From idea to installed, in three steps

1
Build
Create an app, choose the permissions it needs, and test it against your own dev store.
2
Submit for review
Send it for review from the portal. If something needs fixing, you'll see exactly what — in the same place you submitted it.
3
Get installed and paid
Merchants install your app from the catalogue. Watch installs and earnings from your dashboard's home.
